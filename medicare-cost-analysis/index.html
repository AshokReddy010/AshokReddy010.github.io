    How this was made

    Data. CMS "Medicare Inpatient Hospitals – by Provider and Service": for each hospital and DRG, the number of Medicare fee-for-service discharges and the average covered charges, total payment and Medicare payment. CMS suppresses cells with fewer than 11 discharges, so very small volumes are absent.

    Billed vs paid. Covered charges are the hospital's list prices. Medicare does not pay them: it pays a fixed amount per DRG, adjusted for local wages, teaching and low-income patient share, and outliers. The gap between the two is a measure of chargemaster markup, not of what anyone owes.

    Case-mix adjustment. For each year and DRG, the national average per discharge is the yardstick. A hospital's index is its actual dollars divided by the yardstick dollars for its own DRGs and volumes, so hospitals and states are compared like for like.

    Pipeline. A GitHub Actions workflow downloads every release, normalises column names that changed between years, builds dbt models in DuckDB with tests (keys, ranges, reconciliation of every discharge and dollar), and publishes these files. Nothing on this page is typed by hand.
