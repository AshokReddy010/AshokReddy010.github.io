Data Validation Framework

Three systems hold the same transactions and should agree before any report goes out. This framework checks that they do, every day, and names the exact records behind each mismatch. I planted six errors in invented data, and the checks found all six with the right reason, with no false alarms on 113 clean days. A live console rebuilds 60 days of three simulated systems every morning, injects random faults the checks are not told about, and scores whether every one was caught with the right reason.

Data Contract Guard

A Python tool and GitHub Action that enforces data contracts written in YAML: it validates CSV, Parquet and JSON files against column, key, range and business-rule promises, drafts a contract from sample data, and classifies contract changes as breaking or safe with the version bump each needs. A browser playground runs the real package with Pyodide.

Do ESG Funds Vote Like ESG Funds?

I collected three years of fund voting records from the SEC, 73.8 million votes from 12,416 funds, to test whether funds with ESG in their name vote differently from ordinary funds. They do, a little: the fund house a fund belongs to predicts its votes better than its label.

Same Diagnosis, Different Bill

Every public release of CMS's Medicare inpatient data, 2013 to 2024, modelled in dbt and DuckDB to compare what hospitals bill with what they are paid for the same diagnosis. In 2024 hospitals billed $5.03 for every $1 of payment, and for sepsis the 90th-percentile hospital bills 4.4 times what the 10th does while being paid only 1.8 times as much.

SQL Bench: Text-to-SQL Evaluated by Answers

A benchmark of 50 business questions over a realistic retail warehouse in DuckDB, scoring language models by whether their query returns the right answer. It handles the traps real warehouses have (cancelled orders, refunds, business definitions), runs generated SQL in a sandbox, and reports accuracy with confidence intervals and a breakdown of how wrong answers go wrong.
