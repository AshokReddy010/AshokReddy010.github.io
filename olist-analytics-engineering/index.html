One late parcel undoes a five-star marketplace

Olist connects small Brazilian sellers to large online stores. This dashboard covers its public data for January 2017 to August 2018: about 100,000 orders, their deliveries and reviews, plus 8,000 seller-acquisition leads. Every figure comes from dbt models built and tested in DuckDB.

Delivery against the promised date

Orders grouped by how far from the estimated date they arrived, with the average review and the share of one-star reviews. Most parcels arrive early; the ones that do not are where the bad reviews come from.

Month by month

Orders grew fourfold through 2017, peaking with Black Friday in November. The late-delivery rate spiked in early 2018 and reviews dipped with it.

Marketing leads by channel and the share that signed as sellers.

Which leads to call first

A logistic regression scores each lead on the day it arrives. Tested on the 2,000 most recent leads, ranked into tenths by score.

Do customers come back?

Share of each month's new customers who ordered again in later months. Almost nobody does: growth here is all new customers.

States

Sort by any column. Remote northern and northeastern states wait two to three times as long and review lower.

The 20 largest categories by revenue. Freight is a third of the price on cheap, bulky goods.
