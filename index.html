Stock Market Analysis and Prediction Web App

A full-stack app that tracks NASDAQ and NSE stock prices, charts their trends and forecasts the next 7 days. It combines those forecasts with sentiment from financial news to give a buy, sell or hold recommendation, and includes user sign-in, currency conversion and an admin area.

Problem

Someone checking a stock usually has the price in one place, forecasts in another and the news somewhere else. This app puts all three on one page and turns them into a simple buy, sell or hold signal.

Approach

  • Data: five years of daily closing prices from yfinance, and recent headlines from NewsAPI.
  • Three forecasts, 7 days ahead: a linear regression on the previous two closes and a 5-day rolling mean, an ARIMA(5,1,0) model, and a two-layer LSTM reading 30-day windows.
  • Sentiment: each headline is scored with TextBlob and counted as positive, neutral or negative.
  • Signal: buy when the forecast trends up and positive headlines outnumber negative ones, sell when both point down, otherwise hold.

What I would improve next

  • Score the models on held-out data with walk-forward validation and report the error in dollars. The current score is measured on the same data the models were trained on, so it reads too high.
  • Train each model once and cache it, so a page load does not retrain all three.
  • Swap the general-purpose sentiment scorer for one trained on financial text.
  • Package the app with Docker and host a live demo.
